Privacy Policy: JBC Ticket Printer

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what the JBC Ticket Printer Android app ("the app") does with information. The app is published by JBC Weighing Scale, Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). It is the companion app for the JBC-THP258 Bluetooth ticket printer.

1. Information kept only on your device

The following stays in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. We never receive it:

  • Weighing tickets: weights, ticket numbers, dates and times, product names and the text you enter in the ticket lines (for example party, model or vehicle).
  • Your product list (product master), printer settings, and the header and footer text of your tickets.
  • A dealer logo, if one is chosen from your photo gallery. Only the picture you choose is read.

PDF and Excel exports are created on your device. They go only where you send them with the Android share menu.

2. Information sent to our licence server

To activate and check the app licence, the app sends the following to our server at jbcweighingscale.com:

DataWhy
Licence key and site ID that you enterTo identify your licence
Device ID: a one-way hash (SHA-256) made from the Android device ID, brand, model, board and hardware nameTo bind the licence to this device and prevent misuse

No weighing data, ticket data, product names, contacts, location or files are sent. The data is used only for licence management. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. The licence connection currently uses HTTP, which is not encrypted.

3. Permissions

  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to find and connect to the JBC ticket printer. On Android 12 and later the app declares that it does not use Bluetooth scanning to work out your location.
  • Location (Android 11 and earlier only): Android needs this to scan for Bluetooth printers. The app does not read, store or send your location.
  • Internet: only for the licence check described above.

4. What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No account sign-in, and no collection of name, phone number, email address or contacts.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays until you delete tickets in the app, clear the app data, or uninstall the app. To have your licence record (licence key, site ID and device ID) removed from our server, email us. We will delete it, after which the app can no longer be activated on that device.

6. Children

The app is a business tool for weighing operations and is not meant for children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
